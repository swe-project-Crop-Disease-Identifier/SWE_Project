Crop Disease Identifier is a free web app that helps farmers identify plant diseases from leaf photos. Upload a photo, select your crop, and get the disease name, confidence score, and both natural and chemical treatment options instantly.

Yes, completely free. No registration, no subscription, no hidden charges. Designed specifically for small and resource-limited farmers.

  • Take the photo in natural daylight.

The confidence score shows how certain the system is about the diagnosis. A score of 90%+ means high confidence. Below 60% means the photo quality may be low — try a clearer photo for a better result.

  • Take a clearer, closer photo in good light.
  • Make sure you selected the correct crop type.
  • Compare the listed symptoms with your plant.
  • If unsure, consult a local agricultural officer.

You can take a screenshot of the result page to save or share it. A dedicated share and download feature will be available in a future update.

The suggestions are general guidelines based on common agricultural practices. For chemical treatments, always follow the product label, wear protective equipment and respect the pre-harvest interval. When in doubt, consult a licensed agronomist.

Natural treatment uses organic methods — removing infected leaves, neem oil, crop rotation. Safer for the environment and health.

Chemical treatment uses fungicides or pesticides. Acts faster for severe infections but must be used carefully with proper dosage.

Try natural methods first for mild infections. Use chemicals only when necessary.

As soon as possible. Most plant diseases spread quickly in warm and humid conditions. Early action means less crop loss and lower treatment cost.
