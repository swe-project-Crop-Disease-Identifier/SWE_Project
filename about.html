About Crop Disease Identifier

Crop Disease Identifier is a smart web application designed to help farmers identify plant diseases from leaf photos and get treatment suggestions. It aims to make farming easier, healthier and more productive — especially for small and resource-limited farmers.

Upload or Capture

Take a clear photo of your plant leaf.

Select Crop

Choose the crop type from the dropdown.

Get Result

See the disease name, confidence score and treatment options.

Tips for Taking a Good Photo
  • Use natural light (avoid dark or blurry photos).
  • Focus on the affected leaf part.
  • Make sure the leaf is clean and clear.
  • Take the photo from a close distance.
